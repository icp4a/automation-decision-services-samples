# Decision services files

The archives in this repository contain sample decision services. You can import these samples directly into Decision Designer, which points directly to this directory (see `Building decision services in Decision Designer`[![CP4BA](/resources/cloudpak4ba.svg "IBM Cloud Pak for Business Automation")](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/cloud-paks/cp-biz-automation/26.0.0?topic=software-building-decision-services-in-decision-designer)[![ADS](/resources/ads.svg "IBM Decision Intelligence Client Managed Software")](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/dicms/26.0.0?topic=software-building-decision-services-in-decision-designer)).

The Training sample contains several decision services that introduce you to the main concepts of Decision Intelligence Client Managed Software: business rules, data management, decision diagrams, decision tables, interaction policies and task models. The sample includes a series of decision models that gradually increase in complexity.
